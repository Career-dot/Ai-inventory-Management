FAQ

Common questions

Can I try RFPilot before paying?
Yes — every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. You can draft a full proposal from a real RFP before deciding.
What happens if I go over my RFP limit?
We'll let you know when you're close, and you can upgrade any time. You're never cut off mid-proposal.
Does RFPilot integrate with our existing tools?
Growth and Enterprise plans include integrations with common CRMs, procurement portals, and content repositories. Ask us about your specific stack.
Is our proposal content used to train shared models?
No. Your content library and drafts stay private to your workspace and are never used to train models shared across customers.
